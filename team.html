01 Leadership

The team.

Niall Naidoo

Chief Executive Officer Rorschach

Niall Naidoo is the Chief Executive Officer of Rorschach Innovation Capital, where he leads the firm's overall investment strategy, portfolio development, and long-term capital deployment across Africa. He oversees the firm's three investment verticals: Founder's Fund, Frontier Fund, and Property & Industrialisation, which is grounded in the conviction that disciplined capital and applied technology are the most durable drivers of value on the continent.

Niall brings a track record across the full investment lifecycle. Over a decade at the University of Cape Town, he led the institution's technology spin-out strategy — sourcing, investing in, and actively managing start-ups built from university IP, and executing exits. Prior to joining UCT, he executed sell-mandated transactions and led operational turnarounds in family-led businesses. He is also the founder of MediCoach, Rorschach's most successful portfolio company to date, which came out of his research portfolio. His professional expertise spans corporate finance, private equity, venture capital, innovation management, and management consulting. Niall holds an MBA and is currently completing a PhD in Health Technology Assessments.

niall.naidoo@rorschach.co.za

Francois Oosthuizen

Chief Operating Officer Rorschach

Francois Oosthuizen is the Chief Operating Officer and co-founder of Rorschach Innovation Capital, where he leads the origination of technology ventures and oversees operational execution across the firm's mature business portfolio. He plays a central role in building the firm's investment pipeline — identifying emerging technology opportunities, structuring early-stage positions, and ensuring that investment thesis translates into tangible operational outcomes within established businesses.

Prior to co-founding Rorschach, Francois served as Innovation Commercialisation Manager at the University of Cape Town, where he represented the university as a limited partner and made direct investments into UCT's start-up portfolio — sourcing, evaluating, and supporting ventures built on university IP. Before joining UCT, he founded his own business and successfully exited, giving him firsthand experience on both sides of the investment table. Francois holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronic & Computer Engineering and a Master of Engineering in Engineering Management & Development.

francois.oosthuizen@rorschach.co.za

Earl Chetty

Chief Financial Officer Rorschach

Earl Chetty is the Chief Financial Officer of Rorschach Innovation Capital, where he is responsible for the financial management, reporting, and compliance framework across the firm and its portfolio companies. He oversees capital allocation, financial controls, and investor reporting — ensuring that Rorschach operates with the rigour and transparency expected of an institutional-grade fund.

Earl brings a strong foundation in accounting, with hands-on experience in financial compliance, regulatory reporting, and audit-readiness across multiple business environments. His accounting background gives him a precise understanding of how financial structures are built and stress-tested — an essential capability in a firm that acquires mature businesses and transforms them through active management. Earl holds a Bachelor's Degree in Accounting and Finance, is a Full Member of the ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants), and holds an MBA in Business Administration and Management.

earl.chetty@rorschach.co.za

Carlton Ndhlovu

Chief Technical Officer Rorschach

Carlton Ndhlovu is the Chief Technical Officer of Rorschach Innovation Capital, where he leads the firm's engineering function and directs the technical development of its portfolio companies. He is currently leading software development for both MediCoach and VINIS — two of the firm's active investments — overseeing architecture, product build, and engineering delivery across both platforms.

Carlton joined Rorschach as an intern developer, working alongside Niall Naidoo on early thematic technology projects. He progressed quickly to lead the FIFA injury prediction initiative before co-founding MediCoach and Rorschach Innovation Services with Niall and Francois while completing his studies at UCT. His technical depth, combined with a track record of building production software from the ground up, makes him a rare operator — equally capable of setting engineering strategy and shipping code. Carlton holds a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science from the University of Cape Town and is a member of the Golden Key International Honour Society.

carlton.ndhlovu@rorschach.co.za

Dr. Benji Gwashavanhu

Chief Technical Officer VINIS

Dr. Benji Gwashavanhu holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering and heads the technical work at VINIS on the image- and signal-processing side — driving the inspection and maintenance of infrastructure, and the platform's digital twins.

An accomplished mechanical engineering researcher, he specialises in machine condition monitoring, finite element analysis, data analytics and programming. Backed by a strong academic foundation and extensive research experience, he has a particular interest in advancing optical measurement techniques and their application in engineering diagnostics — improving machine performance and reliability through in-depth fault analysis, precise diagnostics, and proactive, data-driven maintenance strategies.

benji@vinis.io
