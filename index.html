02 What is VINIS

A cloud-native, end-to-end inspection platform — no boots on the ground.

VINIS continuously monitors large or complex geographies through three integrated stages, delivering insight wherever your team already works.

STAGE 01

Data fusion

Ingests multispectral satellite imagery and fuses it with auxiliary sensor feeds into a single, standardised picture of your terrain.

STAGE 02

Machine vision

Detects, classifies and characterises objects and their condition — against the asset or risk profile you define.

STAGE 03

Adaptive delivery

Delivers insight through a customisable dashboard, standardised PDF / Excel reports, or real-time API integration into your systems.
