Grading the turf of a nation, from orbit.

VINIS turns years of satellite imagery — combined with live match data — into a single, comparable measure of field health for every cricket ground. Delivered across five provincial unions, so each can see which grounds are thriving, which are declining, and which need attention, without setting foot on one.

01 The challenge

You can't manage what you can't measure.

A union may be responsible for scores of grounds — cricket clubs, schools and university ovals spread across a province. Turf condition drives playability, safety and cost, yet the only way to assess it has been to send someone to look.

01

Manual, subjective, slow

On-the-ground assessment can't cover every ground before a season — and two inspectors rarely score the same field the same way.

02

No history, no trend

Without a consistent record, a slowly declining outfield looks fine right up until it doesn't.

03

Condition without cause

Seeing that a field is worn is one thing — knowing it's from a heavy run of play is what tells you how to fix it.

02 How it works

From raw pixels to a field-health score.

Every ground is watched by a stack of satellites and reduced to one number you can track over time.

STAGE 01

Multi-sensor capture

Sentinel-2 and Landsat 8/9 revisit each ground again and again — hundreds of scenes per site build a deep, five-year-plus history of the turf.

STAGE 02

Spectral indexing

Four indices — NDVI, NDWI, BSI and NDRE — pull vegetation vigour, moisture, bare-soil exposure and early plant stress out of every image.

STAGE 03

Scoring & trend

The indices fuse into a single 0–100 condition score, benchmarked against a 5-year mean, with a per-year trend and automatic anomaly flags.

04 Operational data · MediCoach Smart Club

The field knows how hard it's been played.

We partnered with MediCoach Smart Club to feed live scorecards into the field-health model — every ball bowled and run scored becomes a measure of load. Satellite tells you a field's condition; MediCoach tells you why.

Field intelligence

Load, true asset quality, and automated maintenance job cards.

Load

Balls bowled, overs and running between the wickets convert into a load index — how much wear the play is actually putting on the surface.

Asset quality

Satellite condition set against play load gives the true quality of each ground as an asset — not just how it looks, but how it's holding up.

Maintenance support

High load plus declining condition automatically raises maintenance job cards — the right intervention, on the right ground, at the right time.
