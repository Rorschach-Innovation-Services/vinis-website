From orbit to operations — one intelligence pipeline.

03 Digital twins & simulation

Model failure before it happens.

A living twin of every asset.

VINIS fuses satellite observation with live IoT telemetry to build a continuously updated digital twin of each monitored asset — then simulates its failure modes to predict where and when it will fail.

Sensor + satellite fusion

Strain, temperature, vibration, tilt and load from IoT sensors are merged with overhead imagery into one virtual model of the real asset.

Failure-mode simulation

Simulate structural fatigue, corrosion, thermal stress and vegetation encroachment against real conditions to find the mode most likely to fail first.

Predict, then prevent

Remaining-useful-life estimates and early warnings drive predictive maintenance — you intervene before an outage, not after.
