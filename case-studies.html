CASE 01 Sports turf · Field health

Field-health monitoring for South Africa's cricket unions

Satellite NDVI MediCoach load 5 unions
The challenge

Cricket unions manage many grounds across a region, but assessing turf condition by hand is slow, subjective, and can't cover every ground ahead of a season — and it never explains why a field is worn.

The VINIS approach

VINIS derives a composite field-health score (0–100) from satellite imagery, then fuses in MediCoach Smart Club scorecards to turn play into load — and auto-issues maintenance job cards. Delivered across five unions: KZN Coastal, KZN Midlands, Gauteng, Pretoria and Western Cape.

What it delivers
  • A field-health score per ground, tracked as a time-series
  • Play load from MediCoach scorecards mapped onto condition
  • Automated job cards — rest, irrigation, topsoil — to restore health

CASE 02 Mining · Tailings safety

Tailings-dam compliance & safety in South Africa

Satellite Drone Sensors AI · compliance
The challenge

Tailings storage facilities are among the highest-consequence structures in mining — yet routine oversight falls to SHEQ managers, not geotechnical engineers. Qualified inspectors are scarce, manual inspection is subjective, and audits are too infrequent to catch overtopping.

The VINIS approach

VINIS combines satellite, drone, sensors, manual inspection and AI into one calibrated, cross-validated evidence base — with an append-only ledger and automated GISTM reporting that keeps the facility compliant and safe.

What it delivers
  • Five monitoring streams fused, aligned to how dams actually fail
  • Objective, standardised, higher-frequency evidence
  • A tamper-evident audit trail that writes the compliance report

CASE 03 Healthcare · Clinical systems

Mapping the clinical system in Namibia

Geospatial AI classification Referral network Patient journey
The challenge

To manage a patient's clinical journey you first have to know the clinical system — which hospitals, clinics and GP networks exist where, and which conditions each actually treats. Across a country, that map is fragmented or missing.

The VINIS approach

VINIS identified and classified Namibia's clinical system — geolocating hospitals, clinics, health centres and GP networks and mapping the disease conditions each treats — into one system-of-record of the healthcare landscape.

What it delivers
  • A geolocated registry of 310+ facilities — 55 hospitals, 229 clinics, 26 health centres and GP networks
  • Each facility mapped to the conditions and services it treats
  • A referral network and patient-journey foundation — the right facility, at the right time
CASE 04 Environmental · Waste & compliance

Landfill monitoring in South Africa

Satellite Volumetrics Compliance
The challenge

Landfill operators must track site extent, remaining airspace, encroachment and environmental compliance across large sites that change constantly — hard to keep current with periodic ground surveys.

The VINIS approach

VINIS monitors the site from orbit — measuring footprint and volumetric change over time, detecting unauthorised encroachment and vegetation or thermal anomalies, and generating compliance-ready reports.

What it delivers
  • Footprint & airspace-consumption tracking over time
  • Encroachment and surface-anomaly detection
  • Standardised PDF / Excel reports for regulatory compliance
